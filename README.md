# 1Vision365 Support

This is the public support channel for the Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central apps built by [1Vision365](https://1vision365.com), for example [1Vision IoT Link](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/PUBID.1vision3651683641940304%7CAID.1vision365-iotlink-1%7CPAPPID.fa4f9866-4d79-4fa7-a481-7fb14326c481) and [1Vision IoT-2-PrintVis Connector](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/PUBID.1vision3651683641940304%7CAID.1vision365-iot2printvis-1%7CPAPPID.c837b4e0-f4cb-4a7e-b28f-6c9391cfbd67) . Use it to ask questions, report problems and suggest improvements.

## How to get help

1. **Check the [documentation](https://1vision365.github.io/docs/)** and search [existing issues](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue), your question may already be answered.
2. **[Open a new issue](../../issues/new/choose)** and pick the form that fits:
   - **Bug**: something does not work as expected
   - **Question**: you need help with installation or usage
   - **Feature request**: you would like the app to do something it does not do today
3. Fill in every required field. App version, Business Central version and the exact error message save a lot of back-and-forth.

## Please do not post sensitive data

This repository is **public**. Anyone on the internet can read your issue, including attachments and edit history.

Never include:

- passwords, API keys, tokens or connection strings
- tenant IDs, environment URLs or company names you do not want to be public
- customer, supplier or employee data, or screenshots that show them

If you posted something sensitive by accident, edit or delete the message right away and let us know. Treat anything that was visible as exposed and rotate the affected credentials.

## What happens after you open an issue

- An **automated assistant** may reply first. It answers from our public documentation, can make mistakes, and hands over to a human when it is unsure or when a question needs a person (for example pricing, contracts or anything specific to your environment).
- A **team member** reviews issues and takes over when needed. If the assistant's answer does not solve your problem, reply in the issue and say so.
- Issues from accounts we do not know yet may wait for manual review before they get a reply.
- This channel is not an emergency line and does not come with guaranteed response times, unless your agreement with us says otherwise.

## Security vulnerabilities

Do **not** report security problems in a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to report them privately.

## Community rules

- Be respectful and keep to the topic. Write in English.
- One problem per issue.
- Spam, abuse and off-topic content will be removed, and the accounts behind them may be blocked.
